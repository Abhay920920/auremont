# RARE NUTS / AUREMONT — Plain Language Dictionary

> **Brand Tone Standard**: *Simple + Premium, Never Cheap + Basic.*  
> Our goal is to preserve the prestige and luxury heritage of RARE NUTS while ensuring every customer across India—including first-time online shoppers, older customers, and customers from tier-2 and tier-3 cities—can effortlessly understand, navigate, and purchase without language barriers.

## 1. Master Word & Phrase Dictionary

| Difficult / Formal Term | Preferred Simple Term | Why / Indian Consumer Context |

| **Bespoke** | **Custom / Custom-made / Personalized** | "Bespoke" is a British Savile Row tailoring term rarely understood in everyday Indian English. "Custom" or "Personalized" retains premium status with zero confusion. |
| **Artisanal** | **Handcrafted / Traditional / Expertly Crafted** | French loanword that confuses non-native English speakers. "Handcrafted" conveys premium, small-batch care intuitively. |
| **Curated** | **Carefully Chosen / Handpicked / Specially Selected** | Art-gallery jargon overused in modern marketing. "Handpicked" or "Carefully chosen" feels warmer and clearer. |
| **Curation / Curations** | **Selection / Collection / Gift Boxes** | Abstract museum nouns. "Collection" or "Selection" is concrete and easily grasped. |
| **Complimentary** | **Free / Included Free** | Frequently misconstrued as "compliments/words of praise". "Free" or "Included Free with your order" eliminates doubt. |
| **Recipient** | **Receiver / Person Receiving the Gift / Send To** | Formal legal/postal terminology. In gifting, "Person receiving the gift" or "Receiver's Name" is instantly clear. |

## 3. Contextual Writing Guidelines

### A. Gifting & Custom Gift Boxes
- Say **"Custom Gift Box"** or **"Personalized Gift Box"** instead of *"Bespoke Curation"*.
- Say **"Person Receiving the Gift"** or **"Receiver's Name"** instead of *"Recipient"*.
- Say **"Included Free"** or **"Free Gift Card"** instead of *"Complimentary Gift Card"*.
- Say **"Add Your Personal Message"** instead of *"Personalize your bespoke inscription"*.

### B. Checkout & Payments
- Use **"Delivery Address"** instead of *"Shipping Address"* or *"Postal Address"*.
- Use **"Checkout"** on buttons instead of *"Proceed to Checkout"*.
- Use **"Pay Now"** instead of *"Initiate Payment"*.
- Use **"Apply Coupon"** instead of *"Redeem Coupon"*.
- Clearly explain shipping costs: **"Free Express Delivery"** instead of *"Complimentary Vault Dispatch"*.

### C. Order Tracking & Delivery
- Say **"Preparing Your Order"** instead of *"Order In Fulfilment"*.
- Say **"Dispatched / Sent via Express Courier"** instead of *"Vault Dispatch Initiated"*.
- Say **"Expected Delivery Date"** instead of *"Estimated Fulfilment Window"*.

### D. Errors & User Guidance
- Never show raw technical terms (*unauthorized*, *forbidden*, *internal server error*, *endpoint error*, *payload error*).
- Tell the user what happened in plain human words and provide a clear next step (e.g., *"We couldn't confirm your payment. Please check your card details or try UPI."*).

## 4. Indian English Best Practices
1. **Prefer "Delivery Address" over "Shipping Address":** Indian e-commerce users are conditioned by Amazon India, Flipkart, and quick-commerce platforms to look for "Delivery Address".
2. **Clear Currency & Pricing:** Always present values with the official Rupee symbol (`₹`), and clarify tax status simply (*"Inclusive of all taxes"* or *"Taxes included"*).
3. **Short Sentences:** Keep sentences under 15 words wherever possible. Avoid complex clauses with semicolons or archaic conjunctions.
4. **Active Voice:** Prefer *"We roast our almonds in small batches"* over *"Our almonds are meticulously roasted in convective chambers"*.
