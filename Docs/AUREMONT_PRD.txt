Product Requirements Document (PRD)

AUREMONT – Premium Luxury Almond E-commerce Website

Version: 1.0

Author: Product Manager

Product Type: Luxury Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) E-commerce Platform

Platform: Responsive Web (Desktop + Mobile)

Brand: AUREMONT

------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Executive Summary

AUREMONT is a premium luxury dry fruit brand specializing in
high-quality California almonds. The website aims to provide a premium
shopping experience that reflects elegance, trust, exclusivity, and
craftsmanship.

Unlike traditional grocery websites, AUREMONT focuses on luxury branding
similar to Apple, Rolex, or premium perfume brands where storytelling
and experience are equally important as purchasing.

The platform should deliver: - Luxury visual identity - Fast shopping
experience - Mobile-first responsiveness - Secure checkout - Premium
customer experience - Easy product discovery - High conversion rate

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Product Vision

To become India’s most trusted premium dry fruit brand by delivering a
luxury online shopping experience that makes buying almonds feel as
premium as purchasing luxury fashion or fine jewelry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. Business Goals

Primary Goals: - Increase online sales - Increase repeat customers -

Secondary Goals: - SEO visibility - Email subscriber growth - Wishlist
