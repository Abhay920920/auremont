AUREMONT UI/UX DESIGN BRIEF

Version: 1.0 Product: AUREMONT Luxury Almond E-commerce Website

================================================== 1. DESIGN VISION
==================================================

Create a premium luxury e-commerce experience inspired by high-end
brands such as Apple, Rolex, Louis Vuitton, and premium perfume brands.
Every interaction should communicate elegance, trust, exclusivity,
craftsmanship, and quality.

Core Design Principles - Minimal yet luxurious - Product-first
experience - Rich photography - Elegant typography - Smooth
micro-interactions - High readability - Mobile-first responsive design

================================================== 2. DESIGN MOOD
==================================================

Brand Personality - Premium - Sophisticated - Warm - Natural -
Timeless - Trustworthy - Exclusive

Keywords Luxury Elegant Minimal Premium Modern Organic Refined High-end

==================================================

================================================== 7. SHADOWS
==================================================

Minimal soft shadows Use subtle elevation only. Gold glow reserved for
hover/focus states.

================================================== 8. COMPONENT STYLE
==================================================

Buttons - Filled Gold (Primary CTA) - Outline Gold (Secondary CTA) -
Rounded corners - Height 48px desktop / 44px mobile

Cards - Dark background - Thin gold border on hover - Large imagery -
Spacious padding

Navigation - Transparent over hero - Sticky after scroll - Minimal
icons - Gold logo

Forms - Dark inputs - Gold focus state - Clear validation - Large touch
targets

Product Cards - Large product image - Product name - Price - CTA
button - Hover lift animation

Badges - Premium - Best Seller - Natural - New

Icons - Thin line style - Gold accent - Consistent 24px sizing

================================================== 9. IMAGERY
==================================================

depth of field

================================================== 14. UI PRINCIPLES
==================================================

-   Showcase products before text
-   Keep layouts clean
-   Use whitespace generously
-   Limit accent color to gold
-   Ensure consistent spacing
-   Prioritize readability
-   Maintain premium visual hierarchy
-   Keep interactions predictable
