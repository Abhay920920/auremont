## 🔒 Data Protection & Compliance Principles

1. **Lawful Basis & Consent**: Analytics tracking complies with explicit cookie consent and user opt-in preferences.
2. **Data Minimization**: Collects only necessary product IDs, quantities, and numeric subtotal amounts.
3. **No Sensitive PII Transmission**: User passwords, JWT tokens, credit card CVVs, and complete street addresses are **NEVER** sent to analytics providers.
4. **Data Retention Schedule**:
   - Outbox event logs (`outbox_events`): 90 days.
   - GA4 event records: 14 months (Standard Google Analytics retention).
   - Order history (`orders`): Retained per statutory tax compliance requirements.
5. **Right to Erasure (GDPR / DPCM)**: User profile deletion requests purge personal account records while maintaining anonymized transactional tax records.
