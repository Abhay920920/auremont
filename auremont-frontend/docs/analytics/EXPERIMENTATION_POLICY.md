# RARE NUTS — Conversion Experimentation & A/B Testing Policy

**Purpose:** Defines rules for conducting conversion rate optimization (CRO) experiments without devaluing the **RARE NUTS** luxury brand identity.

---

## 🧪 Experimentation Framework Protocol

### 1. Mandatory Experiment Pre-Requisites
No CRO experiment may launch without:
- **Formulated Hypothesis**: (e.g. "Highlighting 24k gold laser engraving on Product Detail Page will increase Bespoke Gift Box add-to-cart rate by 15%").
- **Primary Metric**: Explicitly defined conversion event (e.g. `add_to_cart`).
- **Control & Variant Groups**: 50/50 randomized split.
- **Minimum Duration**: 14 days (to account for day-of-week purchase variance).

### 2. Strict Brand Safety Boundaries
- 🚫 **No Cheap Countdown Timers**: Aggressive scarcity popups or fake countdown clocks are strictly prohibited.
- 🚫 **No Automated Discount Blasts**: Avoid site-wide discount banner popups that degrade brand equity.
- ✅ **Focus on Value & Heritage**: Experiment with trust badges, harvest origin stories, and luxury gift presentation.
