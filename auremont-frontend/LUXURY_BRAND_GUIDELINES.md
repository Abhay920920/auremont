# Luxury Brand Guidelines

## Voice & Tone
- Confident, not boastful.
- Brief, not verbose.
- We say "Discover" instead of "Click Here".
- We say "The Collection" instead of "All Products".

## Imagery Standards
- **Lighting**: Cinematic, moody, directional lighting.
- **Subject**: Sharp focus on the product, deep bokeh for backgrounds.
- **Color Grading**: Warm, rich tones. Desaturated shadows. 

## Avoid
- Exclamation marks (!).
- "Buy Now" panic inducing language.
- Cluttered UI badges ("SALE", "HOT", "50% OFF") – if a discount is applied, it should be presented elegantly.
