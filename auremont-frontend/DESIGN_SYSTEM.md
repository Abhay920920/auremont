# RARE NUTS Design System

## Core Philosophy
RARE NUTS's design system is built upon the principles of restraint, elegance, and extreme high quality. 

- **Luxury**: No visual clutter. Every element must justify its existence.
- **Timeless**: Avoiding trendy design choices in favor of classic layouts.
- **Cinematic**: Using large-scale imagery and smooth, 60fps animations to tell a story.

## Color Palette
* **Matte Black (`#0B0B0B`)**: Deep, rich background. Not pure `#000000` to reduce eye strain and give a matte finish.
* **Surface Black (`#121212`)**: For cards and slightly elevated elements.
* **Ivory / Warm White (`#FAFAFA`)**: Primary text color. Softer than pure white.
* **Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: The primary brand accent. Used sparingly for buttons, icons, and subtle borders.
* **Soft Bronze (`#A57C1B`)**: Used for hover states and depth.
* **Stone Gray (`#2A2A2A`)**: For borders and subtle dividers.

## Typography
* **Primary (Serif):** Cormorant Garamond. Elegant, high-contrast serif for headings.
* **Secondary (Sans-serif):** Inter. Clean, highly legible sans-serif for body copy and UI elements.

## Spacing & Grid
* **8px System**: All margins and paddings must be multiples of 8 (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128).
* **High Whitespace**: We favor extreme whitespace over tight packing. Elements need room to breathe.
